About Us

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Welcome to Spin The Wheelly — the world's friendliest free random picker. We make decisions fast, fair, and genuinely fun for everyone.

Our Story

Spin The Wheelly was born out of frustration — the classic "you pick" debate that drags on for twenty minutes while everyone politely refuses to make a decision.

We wanted a tool that was instant, visual, and fair. No signups, no ads, no downloads, no "premium" tiers hiding behind a paywall. Just open the page, type your options, and spin. So we built it. Then we made it beautiful — with satisfying animation, premium confetti, and a victory melody that makes every spin feel like a mini celebration.

Our Mission

To make random decisions fast, fair, and genuinely fun for anyone, anywhere, on any device — without asking for an email, a login, or a single cent.

  • Fast — no app to install, no account to create.
  • Fair — cryptographically secure randomness, verified in your browser.
  • Fun — confetti, sound effects, and a wheel that actually feels alive.
  • Accessible — works on phones, tablets, laptops, and smart TV browsers.

Our Promise

We keep Spin The Wheelly 100% free, forever. No ads, no subscriptions, no data collection, no tricks.

  • Never signup — open the page, spin, done.
  • Never sell your data — everything runs locally in your browser.
  • Never paywall features — every wheel tool on the site is free.
  • Never track you — we don't run analytics on your entries or results.

How It Works

There are just three steps — and one of them is "click spin":

  1. Add your options — Type one entry per line. Names, choices, restaurants, whatever you need to pick from.
  2. Hit SPIN — Watch the wheel do its thing with a smooth, suspenseful animation.
  3. Reveal the winner — The wheel lands on a random result, and the celebration begins.

That's it. No settings to configure, no account to activate, no notifications to dismiss.

Privacy First

Your entries and results never leave your device. Every spin happens in your browser, on your computer, using your own hardware.

  • No servers store your entries
  • No images uploaded to the cloud
  • No results tracked or shared
  • No cookies for tracking, ever

The only data we keep is a tiny local preference (light or dark mode), stored safely in your browser and never sent anywhere.

Who Uses Spin The Wheelly

The wheel is loved by all kinds of people:

  • Teachers — pick students, split teams, choose topics.
  • Parents — settle family debates without arguments.
  • Event planners — run raffles, giveaways, and prize draws.
  • Streamers — spin on camera for giveaways and dares.
  • Teams — assign tasks, pick speakers, decide lunch spots.
  • Anyone — who's tired of hearing "I don't know, you pick".
