About Us

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Welcome to Spin The Wheelly — the world's friendliest free random picker. We make decisions fast, fair, and genuinely fun for everyone.

Our Story

Spin The Wheelly was born out of frustration — the classic "you pick" debate that drags on for twenty minutes while everyone politely refuses to make a decision.

We wanted a tool that was instant, visual, and fair. No signups, no downloads, no "premium" tiers hiding behind a paywall. Just open the page, type your options, and spin. So we built it. Then we made it beautiful — with satisfying animation, premium confetti, and a victory melody that makes every spin feel like a mini celebration.

Our Mission

To make random decisions fast, fair, and genuinely fun for anyone, anywhere, on any device — without asking for an email, a login, or a single cent.

  • Fast — no app to install, no account to create.
  • Fair — cryptographically secure randomness, verified in your browser.
  • Fun — confetti, sound effects, and a wheel that actually feels alive.
  • Accessible — works on phones, tablets, laptops, and smart TV browsers.

Our Promise

We keep Spin The Wheelly completely free — supported by unobtrusive advertising and occasional affiliate links so you never have to pay a cent to use it.

  • Never signup — open the page, spin, done.
  • Never sell your wheel data — everything you type stays in your browser.
  • Never paywall features — every wheel tool on the site is free.
  • Never compromise on fairness — the wheel always uses cryptographically secure randomness.

How It Works

There are just three steps — and one of them is "click spin":

  1. Add your options — Type one entry per line. Names, choices, restaurants, whatever you need to pick from.
  2. Hit SPIN — Watch the wheel do its thing with a smooth, suspenseful animation.
  3. Reveal the winner — The wheel lands on a random result, and the celebration begins.

That's it. No settings to configure, no account to activate, no notifications to dismiss.

Privacy First

The content you spin for never leaves your device. Every spin happens in your browser, on your computer, using your own hardware.

  • No servers store your entries
  • No images uploaded to the cloud
  • No results tracked or shared
  • Your theme preference (light or dark) is stored locally in your browser

To keep the site free, we do use third-party analytics (Google Analytics) and advertising partners. These collect limited technical data — such as browser type, device, and pages visited — to help us improve the site and show relevant ads. We never see, store, or share the specific options you spin for. For the full picture, see our Privacy Policy.
