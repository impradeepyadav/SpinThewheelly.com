How to Use It (It's Really Easy)

You don't need a tutorial for this, but here is the quick rundown anyway:

  1. Make your list: Type your choices in the text box on the right. One option per line. (Pizza, Burgers, Sushi — whatever you're craving.)
  2. Hit the button: Click the big SPIN button in the middle of the wheel.
  3. Find out the answer: Watch the wheel spin, hold your breath for a second, and see where the pointer lands.

If you want to keep playing, just turn on the "Auto-remove winner" setting in the menu. This is perfect for things like picking multiple winners for a raffle or assigning tasks to different team members one by one.

Tips for the Best Experience

A few small things can make your spin sessions smoother:

  • Keep labels short. Around 15 characters or fewer fits best on the wheel. Longer text still works — we auto-shrink and wrap it — but shorter is prettier.
  • Use fewer than 20 entries for parties. Too many slices make it hard to read, though up to 50 is supported.
  • Toggle "Segment borders" in Settings to add subtle divider lines between slices.
  • Turn on "Auto-remove winner" for raffles — each spin removes the winner so you can draw multiple winners easily.
  • Try fullscreen mode for classroom or party use — the wheel fills the screen and looks great on a projector.
  • Use images for movie posters, team logos, or food — just click the image button in the toolbar.

Is It Actually Fair? (Behind the Scenes)

Yes. And we take that seriously. We don't use basic math to pick the winner; we use your browser's built-in crypto.getRandomValues API. This is the same level of cryptographic security used to keep passwords safe.

How the randomness works

When you click SPIN, here's exactly what happens:

  1. The browser generates a cryptographically secure random number between 0 and the number of entries.
  2. That number maps directly to a slice on the wheel.
  3. The wheel spins a randomized number of turns and lands on the pre-selected slice.

That means there are no hidden weights, no algorithms favoring certain options, and no patterns. The winner is chosen the millisecond you click spin. The animation is just for the drama. Every single option has a completely equal chance of winning.

Verified fair

Because the random number is picked before the wheel spins, we can't "cheat" the result to make a certain slice land under the pointer. The visual spin always matches the pre-selected winner. No tricks.
