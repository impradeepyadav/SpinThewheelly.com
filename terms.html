4Randomness & Fairness

Every spin uses the browser's built-in cryptographically secure random number generator (crypto.getRandomValues). Each entry has an equal chance of being selected on every spin, and each spin is independent of the last.

The winner is selected before the wheel starts spinning — the animation is purely visual suspense. The result is final for that spin.

Important: we cannot control which entry wins, and neither can you. Please don't use the wheel for high-stakes legal, financial, or medical decisions. It's a fun tool for everyday choices — not a source of professional advice.
