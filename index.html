Spin the Wheel — Free Random Name Picker & Decision Maker

Paste your names or options, spin the wheel, and get a fair, unbiased answer in seconds. No signup, no ads, no downloads.

What is this tool?

Spin the Wheel is a free online spinner wheel. Type in a list of names or choices — one per line — and hit SPIN. The wheel slows down dramatically and lands on a random pick. That's it. No logins, no emails, no complicated setup.

Under the hood it uses your browser's built-in crypto.getRandomValues — the same cryptographically secure random number generator used by password managers and banking apps — so every result is genuinely random.

How to use it

  1. Type your entries. Add one name or option per line in the text box. Paste a whole list at once, or use emojis and images instead of plain text.
  2. Customize it (optional). Open Settings to pick a color theme, adjust the wheel size, change the spin speed, choose volume, or enable background music.
  3. Spin. Click the SPIN button in the middle of the wheel, or use Ctrl+Enter. You can also spin from fullscreen mode.
  4. See the result. A popup shows the winner with a burst of confetti. You can remove the winner and spin again to draw more prizes.

Common ways people use it

  • Classroom name picker. Teachers use it to call on students fairly. Turn on "Remove winner" so every student gets a turn.
  • Raffle and giveaway picker. Project the wheel on a screen and draw winners live. Transparent and impossible to rig.
  • What to eat. Load your favorite restaurants and let the wheel settle the argument. Try the What to Eat wheel.
  • Yes or No decisions. Binary questions work great. The Yes / No Wheel comes preloaded with answers.
  • Team generator. Split a group into random teams. The Random Team Generator handles larger groups.
  • Party games. Truth or Dare, movie night genre, "who pays the bill" — anything where a coin flip feels too simple.

Tips for teachers and event organizers

  • Fullscreen mode projects the wheel cleanly for a room.
  • Large wheel size + large text makes names readable from the back row.
  • Remove winner automatically ensures no student or participant is called twice during the same round.
  • Save your list using the File button so you can reload the same class or contest later.
  • Results history keeps a log of everyone picked so far — useful for raffle announcements.

Privacy and offline use

Everything runs 100% in your browser. Your names, lists and results never leave your device — nothing is sent to a server. Once the page has loaded, you can turn off Wi-Fi and keep spinning. That makes it safe for classrooms with strict privacy policies and events with unreliable internet.

Frequently asked questions

Is Spin the Wheel really free?
Yes — completely free, no ads, no premium tier, no signup. Use it as often as you like.

Is the result actually random?
Yes. Every spin uses crypto.getRandomValues, a cryptographically secure RNG. No bias, no pattern.

How many names can I add?
Up to 50 entries per wheel. For readability, keep labels short — ideally under 15 characters.

Can I change the spin speed?
Yes. In Settings there's a slider from 1.5 seconds (super fast) to 15 seconds (slow and dramatic).

Does it work on phones and tablets?
Yes — the interface is fully responsive and works on any modern device, including phones, tablets, laptops and smart TVs.

Can I use my own images?
Yes. Click the image icon in the toolbar to upload a photo for a specific entry. Images are auto-resized.

Is my data stored anywhere?
No. Everything happens in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

Can I use it as a classroom name picker?
Absolutely — that's one of the most popular uses. Turn on "Remove winner automatically" so every student gets picked once before anyone is picked twice.
