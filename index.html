Fun Facts About Randomness

Randomness is fascinating — here are some fun facts about chance and probability:

  • True randomness is hard — even your brain can't fake it. That's why we use the crypto API.
  • Every spin is independent — landing on "Yes" doesn't make "No" more likely next time.
  • Humans are bad at guessing — we see patterns where none exist.
  • Random decisions reduce bias — perfect for fair choices with multiple options.
  • Coin flips have 50/50 odds — but real coins have a tiny bias.
  • Randomness powers security — the same tech protects your passwords and bank accounts.

The next time you're stuck deciding, remember: a fair random choice is often better than an hour of overthinking.
