About Spin The Wheel: Free Picker Wheel & Random Name Picker

Spin The Wheel is a free online picker wheel and random name picker. Type your names or options, give the wheel a spin, and get a fair answer in seconds. Use it as a name picker for your classroom, a raffle wheel spinner for giveaways, or a quick decision wheel for everyday choices.

Customize Your Experience

In Settings you can customize the wheel exactly the way you want:

Spin duration (custom spin speed)

A slider lets you choose how long the spin lasts, from 1.5 seconds (super fast) to 15 seconds (slow and dramatic).

50+ Premium Color Themes + Custom Colors

From Classic and Neon to Ocean, Sunset, Galaxy and Cyber — pick the palette that matches your event. Or add your own custom wheel colors using the built-in color picker.

Background music

Turn on background music for a more immersive experience. The melody is generated in real time with the Web Audio API — no external files, 100% in your browser.

Winner popup & sound

Customize the winner popup message, show or hide the Remove button, choose between a soft chime or subdued applause, and set the volume from 0% to 100%.

Images & Emojis

Upload photos for each segment or use emojis as entries. Great for visual giveaways or themed wheels.

How to Use the Random Name Picker Wheel

  1. Make your list: Type or paste names/options into the text box — one entry per line. Supports emojis and images.
  2. Customize (optional): Open Settings to change colors, custom palette, spin duration, sound, popup message and more.
  3. Click the SPIN button: Press the center SPIN button (or anywhere on the wheel) or use Ctrl+Enter.
  4. See the winner: Watch the wheel spin and land on a fair random result. Optionally remove the winner and spin again.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Spin The Wheel really free?
Yes! It's 100% free. No premium plans, no ads, no hidden charges — forever. No sign-up required.
Can I customize the winner popup message?
Yes! Open Settings → Display. You can set a custom message, toggle the popup on/off, and show or hide the Remove button.
Can I add my own wheel colors?
Yes! In Settings → Custom Wheel Colors, pick any color and click Add. Custom colors override the current palette and are saved for next time.
Can I control the spin speed?
Yes. Settings has a spin duration slider that goes from 1.5 seconds (very fast) to 15 seconds (very slow).
Is background music available?
Yes! Turn on background music in Settings for a more immersive experience. The melody is generated 100% in your browser.
How do I spin the wheel?
Click the SPIN button in the center of the wheel, or anywhere on the wheel. You can also use Ctrl+Enter.
Is the wheel truly random?
Yes. Every spin uses crypto.getRandomValues — cryptographically secure randomness. Ideal for fair raffles, giveaways and classroom selection.
Do you store my entries?
No. Everything runs locally in your browser. Nothing is ever sent to a server.
Can I use it as a classroom random name picker?
Absolutely. Works great on smartboards and tablets, supports images, and has an optional "remove winner" mode so every student gets a turn.
Is there a free raffle wheel spinner?
Yes — this is a free raffle wheel spinner. Add participant names, spin in front of your audience, optionally remove winners, and download the results list.
